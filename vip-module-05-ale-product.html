MODULE 05

What Agent Lead Engine Actually Sells

Now you understand the buyer. This module covers everything we sell, in plain language, with the value proposition for each piece.

THE BIG PICTURE

We Sell An Agency's Digital Presence, Managed

Most insurance agencies have terrible digital infrastructure. They have a website that hasn't been updated in 5 years, no real CRM (just a spreadsheet or their AMS), no automated follow-up, and they don't show up on Google when someone searches "insurance near me."

We fix all of that, and we manage it for them. They don't hire a marketing person, an IT person, a CRM admin, or a web developer. They pay us a monthly fee and we run it.

PIECE BY PIECE

What Each Piece Does

The CRM

Built on GoHighLevel (a white-label platform). Pre-configured with insurance pipelines, lead capture forms, automated follow-up sequences, and a unified inbox that pulls in calls, texts, emails, Facebook messages, and Google chats. Replaces 5 disconnected tools.

The website

9 conversion-tested templates designed specifically for insurance agencies. Built fast (48 hours for the basic build). Mobile-friendly, SEO-ready, with quote forms that feed straight into the CRM.

Local SEO & Google Business Profile

We manage their Google Business Profile (the listing that shows up when you search "insurance near me"). We post weekly updates, request reviews from their existing customers, and respond to reviews. Goal: rank in the Map Pack.

AI Receptionist

An AI voice that answers their phone 24/7. Books appointments, captures lead info, transfers urgent calls. So when a lead calls at 8 PM and the agent is at dinner, the AI doesn't let the lead bounce.

iMessage / blue-bubble texting

Sends business texts that show up as iMessages (blue bubbles) on iPhones, not green SMS. Massively higher open rates because it looks like a personal text.

Account Manager

A human who manages their account. Updates the website monthly, watches the CRM dashboard, helps optimize. Higher tiers get more dedicated AM time.

48-hour setup

From signup to live system in 48 hours. We do the migration work, not them. This is a major differentiator vs competitors who take 4-8 weeks to onboard.

Integrations

Connects to their AMS (AMS360, EZLynx, HawkSoft, etc.) so customer data syncs both ways. Important: we don't replace their AMS. We sit on top of it.

Mental model for talking about ALE: "We're the agency's digital presence partner. We build, run, and optimize everything that lives outside their AMS — their website, their CRM, their Google profile, their inbound phone, their text marketing. They focus on selling insurance. We make sure leads keep showing up and don't fall through the cracks."
WHO WE'RE NOT
