THE OFFER

What the AI Revenue Leak Audit is

The AI Revenue Leak Audit is a fixed-scope diagnostic engagement for independent insurance agencies with 1 to 25 staff. For one flat price, we spend five days inside your agency's actual workflows — how a lead comes in, how a quote gets followed up, how a renewal gets touched, how information moves between your phone system, your email, your AMS, and your CRM — and we document every place revenue leaks out because a person had to remember to do something.

This is not a software demo and it is not a sales call with a clipboard. You get a written leak map with a dollar figure on each leak, a recorded video walkthrough you can share with partners, and a ranked fix plan with build costs and payback periods. The plan is yours to keep. You can build the fixes with us, with someone else, or not at all. Most agencies have never seen their follow-up gaps measured in dollars before — that measurement is the product.

If we cannot find at least one leak worth more than the audit fee, we tell you on the walkthrough call and refund the fee in full.

THE PROBLEM

The five leaks we find in almost every agency

None of these feel urgent on any given day. Together they cost a typical small agency tens of thousands of dollars a year in lost policies and lost hours.

After 5pm
Leak 1

Calls that go to voicemail

A prospect calls at 5:40pm, gets voicemail, and dials the next agency on the list. You never see the lead at all, so it never shows up in any report.

Day 3+
Leak 2

Quotes nobody follows up

The quote goes out, then follow-up depends on a producer remembering. Every quiet day between quote and follow-up is a day the prospect is talking to someone else.

Hours
Leak 3

Slow first response

Web leads and referrals sit in an inbox for hours before anyone responds. The agency that answers first usually gets the conversation; the rest get voicemail.

X-date
Leak 4

Renewals that start too late

Renewal outreach begins when the renewal notice arrives instead of 60 to 90 days out. By then the client is already shopping, and retention becomes a price fight.

Re-keyed
Leak 5

Data entered twice

The same client details typed into the AMS, the rater, the CRM, and the email thread. Every re-key is staff time burned and a chance for the record to drift wrong.

The point

Your version, in dollars

The audit finds your agency's specific version of these leaks and attaches a yearly dollar figure to each one, so the fix decision is arithmetic, not opinion.

PROCESS

How the 5 days run

  • Day 1: A 45-minute intake call. You walk us through how a lead, a quote, and a renewal actually move through your agency. We get read-only access to the systems you use, or exports if you prefer. Nothing changes in your systems.
  • Days 2 to 4: We do the analysis. We may send 2 or 3 short questions by email. No other time is required from you or your staff.
  • Day 5: You receive the leak map, the recorded walkthrough, and the ranked fix plan, then we do the 30-minute walkthrough call.

Total time required from you: about 75 minutes across the week.

FIT

Who this is for, and who it is not

For: independent P&C agency owners with 1 to 25 staff who suspect manual work is costing them money but have never had the time to measure it. Especially if leads come in by phone and web form, follow-up lives in producers' heads, and renewals get touched only when the notice arrives.

QUESTIONS

What agency owners ask before booking

Do you need access to our systems?

We do not need passwords or logins for the mystery-shop tests: with your written OK, we call your published number and submit your own website form, the same way a customer would. For the deeper analysis, read-only reports or exports you approve are ideal, but screenshots work too. We never change anything in your systems during an audit, and if a system cannot be shared at all, we work from the intake call and staff interviews instead.

Is this a pitch for your CRM in disguise?

No. The deliverables — leak map, recording, fix plan — are yours regardless of what happens next. Some fixes in the plan will not involve us at all: process changes, carrier tools you already pay for, or settings in systems you already own. We say so when that is the right answer.

What if you find nothing?

Then we tell you on the walkthrough call and refund the full fee. The guarantee is written into the engagement: at least one leak worth more than the audit price, or you do not pay.

How is the dollar figure on each leak calculated?

From your numbers where possible: your close rates, your average premium per policy, your call and lead volume, your staff's loaded hourly cost. Where a number cannot be measured directly, we use a conservative range and mark it as an estimate rather than dressing it up as fact.

What happens if we want you to build a fix?

You pick a fix from the ranked plan, we quote the build price before any work starts, and the audit fee credits toward it in full. Builds are scoped and priced separately — the audit commits you to nothing.

Why the founding rate?

This is a new offer. The first 3 agencies get half price in exchange for an honest testimonial if the work earns one, and permission to describe the engagement (anonymized if you prefer) as founding work. Once those 3 are placed, the rate is $1,500.
