Questions owners ask first.

Do you need access to our systems?

We do not need passwords or logins for the mystery-shop tests. With your written OK, we call your published number and submit your website form, the same way a customer would. Read-only reports or exports are ideal, but screenshots work too. We never change anything during an audit. If a system cannot be shared, we use the intake call and staff interviews.

Is this a pitch for your CRM in disguise?

No. The leak map, recording, and fix plan are yours whether or not we build anything. Some fixes will be process changes or use tools you already own. We say so when that is the right answer.

What if you find nothing?

We tell you on the walkthrough call and refund the full fee. The guarantee is simple: at least one leak worth more than the audit price, or you do not pay.

How is the dollar figure on each leak calculated?

From your numbers where possible: close rates, average premium per policy, call and lead volume, and loaded staff cost. Where a number cannot be measured directly, we use a conservative range and mark it as an estimate.

What happens if we want you to build a fix?

You pick a fix from the ranked plan. We quote the build price before work starts, and the audit fee credits toward it in full. The audit commits you to nothing.

Why the founding rate?

This is a new offer. The first 3 agencies get half price in exchange for an honest testimonial if the work earns one, and permission to describe the engagement as founding work, anonymized if you prefer. Once those 3 are placed, the rate is $1,500.
