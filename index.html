Plain answers before you book.

Is this a sales pitch for software?

No. The findings are yours either way, and some fixes in the plan will not involve us at all: process changes, tools you already pay for, settings in systems you own. We say so when that is the right answer.

Do you need access to our systems?

No passwords and no logins. The probe uses your public entry points with your written OK. Deeper analysis uses read-only reports or exports you approve, and nothing in your systems changes.

What if you find nothing?

Then we say so on the walkthrough call and refund the full fee. The guarantee is written into the engagement: at least one leak worth more than the audit price, or you do not pay.

Who is this not for?

Captive agencies, shops with more than about 25 staff, and anyone who wants a report to file away rather than act on. The audit is for owners who will fix what it finds.
