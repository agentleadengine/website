THE MARKET

What the market charges

Audit pricing varies because the word audit can mean very different things. Here is the honest context for an insurance agency principal.

Free audits

Often a vendor sales call with a report attached. The report may be useful, but the vendor is usually trying to sell its product.

$250 to $1,500

Industry benchmark estimate for a productized small-business audit. Scope and depth vary a lot.

$2,000 to $8,000+

Industry benchmark estimate for a consulting-firm assessment. Larger teams and broader strategy work can cost more.

Our offer

$1,500 flat, or $750 for the first 3 agencies. The work has a fixed scope and a written guarantee.

THE WORK

What you are actually paying for

Measured dollar figures

We use your call, lead, quote, renewal, and staff-time data where we can. Each finding shows the math behind it.

A real probe

We call your published number and submit your website form as a customer would. We also review the handoffs between your systems.

Labeled confidence

Every number is marked measured, estimated, or benchmarked. You can see what is solid and what needs a closer look.

A vendor-neutral fix plan

Each fix ends with a plain choice: build it yourself, hire us, or wait. You keep the plan either way.

FIT

When it is not worth it

An honest audit should disqualify some agencies. This is probably not a fit for a captive agency that cannot change its systems, an agency with fewer than 1 or more than about 25 staff, or an owner who wants validation instead of measurement.

It may also be too early if you do not have enough lead, quote, call, or renewal activity to review. The free 15-minute leak check is a good way to find that out before you pay for a full audit.

GUARANTEE

How the guarantee works

We promise to identify at least one revenue leak worth more than the audit fee. We put that promise in writing. If we cannot, we refund the full fee. We do not invent a problem to justify the invoice.

The fee also credits in full toward any build you choose after the audit. The audit does not require you to hire us for the next step.
