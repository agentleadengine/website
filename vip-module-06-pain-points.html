MODULE 06

The 5 Pain Points We Solve

These are the angles you'll use on every cold call. Each one points at real money the agency is leaving on the table. Memorize the numbers.

THE 5 HOOKS

Each Pain Point = A Cold Call Opener

You don't need to use all 5 in one call. Pick the one that fits the moment, lead, or campaign you're calling for.

01
Quote Follow-Up Gap

"Most agencies follow up once and stop"

Industry data: 70% of agencies stop after one follow-up on a quote. Best agencies follow up 5-8 times automatically. Result: a 30%+ lift in close rate.

Cold call line: "When someone requests a quote and doesn't respond right away, how many times does your team follow up?"

02
5-Minute Window

"Speed-to-lead beats price"

Inbound web leads contacted within 5 minutes are 100x more likely to convert vs leads contacted at 30 minutes. Most agencies take hours or days.

Cold call line: "When a quote request hits your website at 7 PM, what happens?"

03
After-Hours Leads

"Leads that come in at night vanish"

Most leads come in evenings and weekends, when no one is in the office. By Monday morning, the lead has called 3 competitors. The first one to respond usually wins.

Cold call line: "What happens to a lead that comes in Saturday at 2 PM?"

04
Invisible Website Visitors

"95% of your visitors leave anonymously"

Out of every 100 people who visit an insurance agency website, fewer than 5 fill out the quote form. The other 95 leave without identifying themselves. We capture them.

Cold call line: "How many people do you think visit your website every month?" (then: "and how many of those become leads?")

05
Referral Plateau

"Referrals stop scaling at some point"

Most agencies grow on referrals until they hit ~$1M in revenue. Then growth flatlines because there's no system to ask, track, or reward referrals. We build the system.

Cold call line: "How are you currently asking your existing customers for referrals?"

BONUS: The Compounding Effect

You don't need to fix all 5. Fixing just ONE typically generates a 10-20% revenue lift. Fixing 2-3 doubles it. We fix all 5 in one platform.
