What we do

Natural stone restoration

Honing, polishing, sealing, and repair for marble, granite, travertine, and slate. Countertops that lost their luster, floors dulled by years of traffic, outdoor stone beaten by Georgia weather: we bring the finish back instead of tearing it out.

Tile & grout cleaning

Water extraction and orbital agitation lift years of dirt out of tile and grout lines. Kitchens, bathrooms, foyers, showers. The floor you're embarrassed by is usually one visit away from the floor you picked out.
