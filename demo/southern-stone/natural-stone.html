Natural stone countertop and floor restoration

Have your home's natural stone surfaces seen better days? Don't let anyone talk you into replacing them. With the right specialist, your marble and granite can be repaired, restored, and kept beautiful for years to come.

Restoration, tailored to your stone

At Southern Stone and Tile Care, we're passionate about breathing new life into aged natural stone. Every home and every surface is different, so we build a plan around yours: a mix of honing, polishing, sealing, stripping, grout staining, caulking, waxing, and resurfacing. Whatever the stone actually needs, and nothing it doesn't.

Once we've assessed the state of your surfaces, you'll get a clear recommendation and a clear price before any work starts.

Indoors and out

Georgia weather is hard on stone. Rain, wind, and sun leave walkways, patios, and pool decks dull, stained, and scratched. You don't get a second chance at a first impression. Exterior stone deserves the same care as your kitchen counters, and we treat it that way.

What stone can we restore?

Restoration is an art and a science. We spend our days on surfaces of every shape and form, including plenty our clients believed were beyond repair.

  • Marble
  • Granite
  • Travertine
  • Slate
  • Limestone
  • Terrazzo
