Tile and grout cleaning and restoration

Dirty, worn floors do more than cramp your style. They drag down the value of your property. Tile and grout restoration isn't an expense; it's an investment in your home.

The Southern difference

We use the industry's most advanced water extraction and orbital agitation techniques to pull surface dirt out of tile and lift stubborn stains from grout, restoring floors to their brand-new glory without harsh acids or damage to the surface.

What we handle

  • Tile deep cleaning: kitchens, bathrooms, foyers, mudrooms, showers
  • Grout cleaning and stain lifting: years of build-up, gone in a visit
  • Grout sealing: lock the lines against the next spill
  • Grout staining and color correction: even out blotchy, aged grout
  • Recaulking: clean, mold-resistant lines around tubs and counters
