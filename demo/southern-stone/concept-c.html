Condition report:
your stone, today.

Cleaning products, food prep, foot traffic, and Georgia weather all take their toll. Marble etches. Granite clouds. Grout darkens. None of that means replacement. It means the surface is due for treatment. Restoration is an art and a science; we've spent 15+ years mastering both.

The good news: you can win the war against dirt build-up, dullness, and defects without spending thousands on new marble and granite.
