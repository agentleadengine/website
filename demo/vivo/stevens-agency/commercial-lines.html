What's included

The lines that keep your business running.

We write the major commercial lines for North Carolina small business, and we package them into a Business Owner's Policy when it makes sense.

General Liability

Coverage for third-party bodily injury, property damage, and personal injury claims. The foundation of any commercial program.

Workers' Comp

State-required coverage for employee injury and lost wages. We pair you with carriers that understand your class code.

Commercial Auto

Trucks, vans, fleet, and hired-and-non-owned auto. Built for contractors, delivery, and any business that puts wheels on the road.

Business Owner's Policy

GL plus property in one package, often with business income coverage built in. The most cost-effective program for small business.

Cyber Liability

Coverage for breach response, data restoration, ransomware, and third-party liability. Increasingly required by clients and lenders.

Industry-Specific

Contractors, restaurants, salons, professional services, trucking, and more. Matched to a carrier that specializes in your trade.

Commercial
questions.

The most common things we get asked by Carolina business owners shopping their commercial program.

Do you write workers' comp for high-mod accounts?

+
Yes. We have markets that work with contractors, manufacturers, and trades businesses with experience mods up to 1.50, plus pay-as-you-go options through carriers like AmTrust and Pie.

How fast can I get a certificate of insurance?

+
Same business day in almost every case, often within an hour during business hours. We hold your file ready for COIs so general contractors and clients are not waiting on us.

Can you handle multiple states for our growing business?

+
Yes, we are licensed across NC, SC, and VA, and we work with national carriers that can write a coordinated program if you operate in additional states.

Do you cover startups and brand-new businesses?

+
Absolutely. We have several carriers that specialize in new ventures with no prior insurance history, and we'll structure your first program so you can grow without re-shopping every six months.
