Commercial Coverage Options

The Lines That Keep Your Business Running

We write the major commercial lines for North Carolina small business, and we package them into a Business Owner's Policy when it makes sense.

General Liability

Coverage for third-party bodily injury, property damage, and personal injury claims. The foundation of any commercial program.

Workers' Compensation

State-required coverage for employee injury and lost wages. We pair you with carriers that understand your class code and audit fairly.

Commercial Auto

Trucks, vans, fleet, and hired-and-non-owned auto. Built for contractors, delivery, and any business that puts wheels on the road.

Business Owner's Policy

GL plus property in one package, often with business income coverage built in. The most cost-effective program for small business.

Cyber Liability

Coverage for breach response, data restoration, ransomware, and third-party liability. Increasingly required by clients and lenders.

Industry-Specific Programs

Contractors, restaurants, salons, professional services, trucking, and more. We match you to a carrier that specializes in your trade.

Why It Matters

The Right Commercial Program
Pays for Itself.

A bad claim, a missing endorsement, a cheap policy that turns out to exclude what you actually do. Most small businesses do not learn the gaps in their program until they are filing a claim. By then, the cost is already out of pocket.

Stevens Agency builds commercial programs the way a CFO would. We map your operations, identify the exposures, and shop the right carriers. You get a policy that pays when you need it, not just one that costs less on the front end.

  • Carrier matched to your industry, we know which carriers love contractors, which ones love restaurants, and which ones to avoid.

  • Workers' comp audit support, we help you prepare for the audit so you are not surprised by a premium bump.

  • Certificates of insurance, fast turnaround on COIs and additional insureds, often the same day.

  • Annual program review, your business changes, and your program should keep pace. We review every year.
