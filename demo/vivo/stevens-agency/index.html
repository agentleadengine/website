Our Story

40 Years of Protecting Families in the Triangle region

Stevens Agency was founded in 1984 by James and Margaret Stevens with one mission: make quality insurance accessible to every family in our community, regardless of where you are in life or the budget you're working with.

Today, we are an independent agency representing 15+ top-rated carriers. That means we work for you, not the insurance company. We compare rates, explain your options in plain language, and help you choose coverage that actually makes sense for your life.

  • Independent agency, we compare 15+ top carriers to find your lowest rate, every time.

  • Responsive service, our agents are licensed across all major lines and respond fast.

  • Free annual policy reviews, your life changes, and your coverage should keep pace with it.

  • Local and always accessible, call, text, visit our Raleigh office, or get a quote online.
