Business Coverage

The Protection Your Business Deserves

From your first employee to your biggest contract, we have coverage that scales with your business, and your ambitions.

General Liability

Covers third-party bodily injury, property damage, and advertising claims against your business. Most landlords and commercial clients require proof of this before you sign a lease or start a job.

Commercial Property

Protects your building, equipment, inventory, and signage against fire, theft, vandalism, and weather damage. Keeps your business running after the unexpected happens.

Workers' Compensation

Required by law in most states, covers medical bills and lost wages when an employee is injured on the job. Protects your team and shields your business from costly lawsuits.

Errors & Omissions (E&O)

Also called Professional Liability, protects service businesses from claims of negligence, mistakes, or incomplete work. Essential for consultants, agents, contractors, and accountants.

Commercial Auto

Covers vehicles used for your business, company trucks, delivery vans, or an employee's car on a work errand. Your personal auto policy will not cover commercial use, period.

Business Owner's Policy (BOP)

Bundles General Liability and Commercial Property into one affordable package, the smart choice for most small businesses. One policy, one payment, maximum protection.
