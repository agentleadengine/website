What's Covered

Six Essential Auto Coverages, Explained Simply

Every driver needs different coverage. Here's what each type protects, and why it matters when you're on the road.

Liability Coverage

Covers injuries and property damage you cause to others in an accident. Required by law in most states, liability pays medical bills, repair costs, and legal fees, protecting your savings from a single costly mistake.

Collision Coverage

Pays to repair or replace your vehicle after a collision, whether you hit another car, a guardrail, or a telephone pole. Especially important if you're financing or leasing, since your lender will require it.

Comprehensive Coverage

Protects your car from damage that isn't caused by a collision, theft, vandalism, hail, flooding, falling trees, and animal strikes. It's the coverage that handles everything the road doesn't throw at you.

Uninsured Motorist

Covers your medical bills and vehicle damage when the at-fault driver has no insurance, or not enough. One in eight drivers on the road is uninsured. This coverage makes sure their mistake doesn't become your financial burden.

Medical Payments

Pays medical expenses for you and your passengers after an accident, regardless of who caused it. Covers hospital visits, surgery, X-rays, and rehabilitation costs. A critical safety net when health insurance has gaps.

Roadside Assistance

Flat tire at midnight? Dead battery in a parking lot? Roadside assistance covers towing, jump starts, lockout service, and fuel delivery, so you're never stranded without help, no matter where you are.

Auto Insurance Discounts You May Already Qualify For

We stack every available discount to bring your premium down. Here are the most common savings our drivers unlock.

Good Driver Discount

A clean driving record with no accidents or moving violations in the past three to five years can reduce your premium by up to 25%. Safe driving is the single biggest money-saver.

Multi-Car Discount

Insuring two or more vehicles on the same policy saves 10-25% per car. Family households with multiple drivers benefit the most from this straightforward discount.

Bundling Discount

Combine your auto and home insurance with one carrier and save 10-25% on both policies. One agent, one bill, and better coverage, it's the easiest discount most families miss.

Good Student Discount

Full-time students under 25 with a B average or higher qualify for up to 15% off. Bring a recent report card or transcript, we'll apply the savings right away.

New Vehicle Discount

New cars with factory-installed safety features, anti-lock brakes, airbags, lane assist, and collision avoidance, qualify for lower premiums with most carriers.

Safe Driver App

Many carriers offer telematics programs that track your driving habits through a smartphone app. Drive safely and earn up to 30% off, the discount grows as your good habits are verified.
