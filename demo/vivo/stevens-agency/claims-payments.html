The Claims Process

How It Works, Step by Step

From the moment an incident happens to the day your claim is fully resolved, we guide you through every step so nothing falls through the cracks.

1

Report the Incident

Call us or your carrier's 24/7 claims hotline as soon as an incident occurs. Early reporting gives your claim the strongest possible start.

2

Document Everything

Take photos, gather police reports, and collect witness contact information. Thorough documentation protects your claim and speeds up the process.

3

File Your Claim

We submit your claim directly with your carrier and verify every detail is accurate, so nothing delays your settlement or gets lost in the process.

4

We Advocate for You

Our team monitors progress and pushes for a fair, timely outcome. You're never left alone navigating the insurance company on your own.

5

Claim Resolved

Payment is issued and repairs are authorized. We follow up to confirm everything was handled correctly before we close the file.

Accident Guide

What to Do Immediately
After an Accident

The actions you take in the first few minutes after an accident can directly affect your claim outcome. Follow these steps to protect yourself, your passengers, and your coverage.

  • Move to Safety, Get your vehicle out of traffic if safe to do so, and turn on hazard lights immediately.

  • Call 911, Report the accident and request a police report, even for minor incidents. Official documentation strengthens your claim.

  • Document the Scene, Photograph all vehicles, damage, road conditions, and visible injuries before anything is moved or altered.

  • Collect Information, Get names, phone numbers, license plates, and insurance details from every driver and witness involved.

  • Avoid Admitting Fault, Do not apologize or accept liability at the scene. Let the claims process and the facts determine responsibility.

  • Call Us Right Away, Dial (516) 780-1385 and we'll guide you through the next steps and begin the claims process immediately.
