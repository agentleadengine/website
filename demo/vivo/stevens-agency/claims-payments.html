How a claim works with us

Five steps, one phone number.

From the first call to the final check, we are on your side and on the phone with the carrier.

01

Call us first

Even before the carrier. We'll make sure the claim is filed correctly the first time and document the right way.

02

We file with the carrier

Same day during business hours. After hours, we connect you with the 24/7 claim line and follow up the next morning.

03

Adjuster assignment

We track the assignment and stay in the loop so nothing stalls. Most adjusters reach out within 24-48 hours.

04

Repair & advocacy

If the offer is low or coverage is denied, we push back. We've seen the playbook before, and we know how to escalate.

05

Resolved & reviewed

Once the claim closes, we review your coverage to make sure nothing similar can happen again. Adjustments on us.
