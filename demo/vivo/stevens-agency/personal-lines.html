Common
questions.

The questions we hear most about personal lines, answered the way we'd answer them on the phone.

Why is my home insurance going up so much in NC?

+
Wind, hail, and reinsurance costs in the Carolinas are pushing rates higher across every carrier. The right move is not to drop coverage, it's to shop. We re-quote your home policy across all 38+ carriers so you stay covered for less.

Can you bundle auto and home with different carriers?

+
Yes, and sometimes it makes sense. We don't force a one-carrier bundle if a split program saves you more. We compare both and recommend whichever wins on price plus claim experience.

Do I really need an umbrella policy?

+
If you own a home, have a teenage driver, or have meaningful net worth, almost certainly yes. $1M of umbrella runs about $20 a month and stacks on top of every underlying policy.

How much can I expect to save by switching?

+
Households that switch to Stevens & Co. typically save 12% to 24% in the first year, and we re-shop every renewal so the savings stick.
