Personal Coverage Options

Coverage for Everything You Drive, Live In, and Love

We write all of the major personal lines through 15+ A-rated carriers, and we look for stackable discounts so the bundle saves you real money.

Auto Insurance

Liability, collision, comprehensive, uninsured motorist, and PIP. We compare carriers on rate and claim experience so you do not get burned at renewal.

Home Insurance

Dwelling, personal property, liability, and loss of use. Built for North Carolina exposures including wind, hail, and water backup.

Renters Insurance

Affordable protection for your belongings, plus personal liability that follows you wherever you live. Most policies start under $20 a month.

Umbrella Insurance

An extra $1M to $5M of liability coverage that sits on top of your auto and home policies. Cheap protection against the unlikely but expensive.

Motorcycle, Boat, and RV

Coverage built around how you actually use your toys. Includes towing, on-water, and roadside in addition to the standard liability and physical damage.

Why Personal Lines Matters

One Bad Day Should Not
Erase Twenty Good Years.

A car accident, a kitchen fire, a tree on the roof, a teenager on the policy. The everyday risk is what wipes families out, not the headline-grabbing disaster. Personal lines coverage is what stands between a rough week and a financial reset.

As an independent agency, we shop your auto, home, and umbrella across 15+ carriers every renewal. You get the right coverage for the right price, without re-quoting it yourself.

  • Bundled discounts, save 12% to 24% by combining auto, home, and umbrella with one carrier.

  • Annual policy reviews, your life changes, and so should your coverage. We re-shop every year.

  • Claims advocacy, when something goes wrong, your Stevens Agency rep is on the phone with the carrier on your side.

  • Local agents, call, text, or stop by the office in Raleigh. Real people who know your file.
