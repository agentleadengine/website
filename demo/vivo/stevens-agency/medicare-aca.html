Medicare & ACA
questions.

What we hear most often during the Medicare and ACA enrollment season.

Should I pick Medicare Advantage or a Supplement?

+
Depends on your doctors, your meds, and how often you travel. Advantage plans are often $0 premium but networked. Supplements cost more monthly but give you near-total flexibility. We compare both side by side.

When can I enroll in Medicare?

+
Initial Enrollment is the 7-month window around your 65th birthday. Annual Enrollment runs October 15 to December 7. Special Enrollment Periods exist for life events (moving, losing coverage, etc). We track your dates.

Will I owe a penalty if I delay Part B or Part D?

+
Possibly, yes. Part B and Part D late-enrollment penalties stick with you for life. We'll walk you through whether you can defer (you have credible coverage at work) or whether you need to enroll now.

Do you charge a fee for Medicare or ACA help?

+
No. We are paid by the carriers, not by you. Our fee structure is identical whether you enroll directly or through us, so working with an independent agent is genuinely free.
