What We Cover

Specialized Coverage for Every Type of Ride

Recreational vehicles have unique risks that standard auto policies don't cover. We match each vehicle type with a carrier that specializes in it.

Motorcycle Insurance

From sport bikes to cruisers, your motorcycle policy covers collision, comprehensive, liability, and uninsured motorist protection. We also offer accessory coverage for custom parts, chrome upgrades, and saddlebags that standard policies leave out.

Boat & Watercraft Insurance

Whether you own a fishing boat, jet ski, or pontoon, watercraft insurance covers hull damage, liability on the water, towing, and fuel spill cleanup. We'll also make sure your trailer and onboard equipment are included in your policy.

RV & Motorhome Insurance

Your RV is part vehicle, part home, and it needs a policy that covers both. RV insurance protects the structure, your personal belongings inside, and liability on the road. Full-timer coverage is available if your RV is your primary residence.

ATV & Off-Road Insurance

ATVs, dirt bikes, UTVs, and side-by-sides face unique off-road hazards that most auto policies exclude entirely. Our off-road coverage includes collision, theft, liability, and medical payments, whether you're on the trail or loading up at the trailhead.

Seasonal coverage options, store smarter, save more

Flexible Protection

Seasonal Coverage Options Available

Most recreational vehicles spend months in storage each year. Why pay full-price coverage during the off-season? We offer lay-up and seasonal policies that scale your premium to match how you actually use your vehicle, so you save money without dropping protection entirely.

Our agents will help you build a coverage plan that adjusts to your riding, boating, or camping season, and still keeps you protected against theft, weather damage, and vandalism while your vehicle is stored.

  • Lay-Up Coverage, reduces your premium during storage months while maintaining comprehensive and theft protection year-round

  • Agreed Value Policies, locks in your vehicle's value upfront so there's no depreciation dispute if a total loss occurs

  • Custom Parts & Equipment, covers aftermarket upgrades, accessories, and modifications that increase your vehicle's value beyond factory spec

  • Roadside & On-Water Towing, 24/7 emergency assistance whether you break down on the highway or need a tow back to the marina

Recreational Insurance Discounts You Should Know About

We shop multiple specialty carriers and apply every available discount, so you get maximum coverage at the lowest possible rate.

Safety Course Completion

Completed a motorcycle safety course, boating certification, or ATV training? Most carriers reward certified riders with 5-15% off their annual premium.

Multi-Policy Bundle

Bundle your recreational vehicle with your auto or home policy for instant savings. One agent, one carrier, one bill, and up to 25% off your combined premium.

Lay-Up / Storage Period

Store your vehicle during winter or the off-season? Lay-up discounts reduce your premium during months your vehicle isn't in use, while keeping theft and weather protection active.

Experienced Rider

Years of riding experience and a clean driving record signal lower risk to insurers. Many carriers offer experience-based discounts for riders with 3+ years and no claims.
