What's included

Coverage for every stage of life.

From young families just starting out to business owners planning for succession, we match you with the right policy for where you are right now.

Term Life

Affordable coverage for 10, 20, or 30 years. Ideal for young families who need maximum protection at the lowest cost while the kids are home.

Whole Life

Permanent coverage that never expires and builds cash value. Premiums stay level for life, and you can borrow against the policy.

Indexed Universal Life

Flexible permanent coverage with cash value tied to a market index. A smart option for high earners looking for tax-advantaged growth.

Key Person

Protects your business if a critical owner, partner, or employee passes away. Covers lost revenue and keeps operations running.

Buy-Sell Funding

Life insurance structured to fund a buy-sell agreement so the business stays in the right hands when an owner passes.

Final Expense

A small whole life policy designed to cover funeral, medical, and end-of-life expenses. Detailed coverage on its own page.

Life insurance
questions.

The questions we hear most about life insurance, answered the way we'd answer them on the phone.

How much life insurance do I actually need?

+
Rule of thumb is 10 to 15 times your annual income, plus mortgage and education costs. We run a free needs analysis to size it right for your specific household.

Term or whole, which one should I get?

+
Most families: term covers the years your kids are home and the mortgage is biggest. Some households also need a small permanent policy for legacy or estate planning. We'll lay out both side by side.

Do I need a medical exam?

+
Many of our carriers offer no-exam options up to $1M for healthy applicants. For larger policies or pre-existing conditions, a paramed exam is usually still required, but we have streamlined-underwriting carriers too.

Can I change my coverage later?

+
Yes. Most term policies are convertible to permanent without a new exam, and we review your coverage annually so it keeps pace with your life.
