What's Covered

Six Layers of Protection for Your Home

A complete homeowners policy covers far more than just your roof and walls. Here's what each coverage type does, and why it matters for your family.

Dwelling Coverage

Pays to repair or rebuild the structure of your home, walls, roof, floors, and built-in appliances, when damaged by a covered event like fire, wind, or hail. This is the foundation of every homeowners policy.

Other Structures

Covers detached garages, fences, tool sheds, and guest houses on your property. Typically set at 10% of your dwelling limit, so if your home is insured for $350,000, you have $35,000 for other structures.

Personal Property

Replaces your furniture, clothing, electronics, and valuables if they're stolen or destroyed. High-value items like jewelry, art, and collectibles may need a scheduled endorsement for full replacement value.

Liability Protection

If a guest is injured at your home or you accidentally damage a neighbor's property, this coverage pays your legal fees and any settlement. Most policies start at $100,000, we often recommend $300,000 for families.

Additional Living Expenses

Pays for a hotel, meals, and temporary housing if your home becomes uninhabitable after a covered loss. Your family stays safe and comfortable while repairs are completed, coverage typically lasts up to 24 months.

Medical Payments

Covers minor medical bills for guests who are injured on your property, regardless of who is at fault. Typically $1,000-$5,000, it keeps small accidents from becoming disputes between friends or neighbors.

Know the Difference

Replacement Cost vs. Actual Cash Value

Most homeowners don't realize that how their policy is written determines how much they actually receive after a claim. This is one of the most important decisions you'll make when buying home insurance, and most agents never explain it clearly.

Actual Cash Value (ACV) pays what your belongings are worth today, after depreciation is deducted. A 12-year-old roof that cost $20,000 to install might only pay $7,000 under ACV. Replacement Cost Coverage pays what it actually costs to rebuild or replace at current prices.

  • Replacement Cost Coverage, pays to rebuild or replace at today's market prices, with no depreciation deducted from your settlement

  • Extended Replacement Cost, adds 20-50% above your policy limit if construction costs surge after a widespread disaster in your area

  • Inflation Guard Endorsement, automatically raises your coverage limit each year to keep pace with rising building material and labor costs

  • We explain every option in plain language, no insurance jargon, no surprises. You know exactly what you're buying before you sign anything

Home Insurance Discounts You May Already Qualify For

We compare rates from 15+ carriers and apply every available discount, so you get the most protection for the least money.

Security System Discount

Monitored alarms, deadbolt locks, smoke detectors, and smart home security systems can reduce your premium by up to 20%. Ask us what qualifies.

New Construction Discount

Newer homes with updated electrical, plumbing, and roofing carry less risk. Insurers reward recently built homes with significantly lower rates than older properties.

Loyalty Discount

Staying with the same carrier for three or more years earns loyalty credits, up to 15% off your annual premium. Consistency pays off in home insurance.

Bundle & Save

Combining your home and auto policies with the same carrier typically saves 10-25%. One agent, one bill, and one call when you need help, it's the easiest savings most families leave on the table.

Claims-Free Discount

Never filed a home insurance claim? That clean record is worth money. Most carriers reward claims-free customers with 5-15% off, and the discount grows the longer your record stays clean.
