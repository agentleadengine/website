SAMPLE, AI Revenue Leak Audit

Maple Grove Insurance Agency (fictional)

This is a sample report. Maple Grove Insurance Agency is a fictional 4-person independent agency on Long Island. Every number below is invented to show what a real audit deliverable looks like, the structure, the math style, the confidence tags, and the fix plan are exactly what a paying client receives.

Deliverable 1 of 3, Leak Map

Dollar figures are estimates built from intake answers and conservative industry benchmarks; they are sized to show scale, not audited to the dollar. Commission math uses $1,800 average annual premium × 12% commission × 4-year average retention ≈ $860 lifetime commission per bound account. Each figure is tagged MEASURED (from their records or our probe), ESTIMATED (their volume × conservative industry rate), or BENCHMARKED (directional only).

Leak 1: After-hours calls go to voicemail and die there

  • What happens today: “If it rings after 5:30 it goes to the machine. Maria checks messages first thing, but half of them don't leave one.” Our probe called at 6:15pm on a Tuesday, four rings, generic voicemail, no text-back, no callback by noon the next day.
  • The math: ~5 after-hours calls/week × 52 × 40% who never reconnect × 25% close rate × $860 = ~$22,400/yr
  • Annual cost, estimated: $22,000
  • Confidence: ESTIMATED (call volume from their phone logs; reconnect and close rates are conservative industry figures). Probe evidence: MEASURED.

Leak 2: Quoted-not-sold follow-up lives in the producer's head

  • What happens today: “Joe sends the quote and follows up if he remembers. There's no list.” No second touch is scheduled for roughly half of all quotes.
  • The math: 25 quotes/mo × 12 × 50% with no 2nd touch × 10-point close-rate lift from one follow-up × $860 = ~$12,900/yr
  • Annual cost, estimated: $12,000
  • Confidence: ESTIMATED (quote volume from their register; lift from published follow-up studies, rounded down).

Leak 3: Web leads answered in hours, not minutes

  • What happens today: Website form sends an email to the office inbox. First response averages 3+ hours during the day; Saturday submissions wait until Monday.
  • The math: 12 web leads/mo × 12 × ~10% of leads lost outright to slow response × 25% close × $860 ≈ ~$3,100/yr
  • Annual cost, estimated: $3,000
  • Confidence: BENCHMARKED (no web-lead outcome records exist, worth instrumenting; excluded from guarantee).

Leak 4: Renewal outreach starts when the notice arrives

  • What happens today: First client touch is the carrier's renewal notice, which is also the client's cue to shop. No 90/60/30-day cadence exists.
  • The math: 650 households × 4% lost at renewal that proactive outreach retains × $860 = ~$22,400/yr
  • Annual cost, estimated: $22,000
  • Confidence: ESTIMATED (book size from AMS export; retention lift is industry benchmark, low end).

Leak 5: Every inbound lead is typed three times

  • What happens today: Website email → spreadsheet → AMS, re-keyed by hand. Staff estimate 6 hours/week across the office, plus the occasional wrong phone number that kills a lead.
  • The math: 6 hrs/week × 52 × $22/hr including taxes and benefits = ~$6,900/yr
  • Annual cost, estimated: $6,900
  • Confidence: MEASURED (hours from staff interviews; loaded cost from their payroll figure).
Total
Estimated annual recoverable leak: ~$66,000 (conservative range $45,000–$85,000). Headline uses the low end of every range.

Deliverable 2 of 3, Findings & Evidence (excerpt)

  • Probe call, Tue 6:15pm: voicemail after 4 rings, no text-back, no callback within 18 hours. (MEASURED)
  • Phone logs, trailing 90 days: 31% of weekday calls arrive after 5pm or before 9am. (MEASURED)
  • Quote register sample (40 quotes): 19 show no recorded second touch. (MEASURED)
  • AMS renewal report: no first-touch date field is populated for any 2026 renewal. (MEASURED)
  • “I think Joe handles those.”, owner, on Saturday web leads. (Interview, instrumented as Leak 3.)

Deliverable 3 of 3, Fix Plan

Ranked by payback period (build cost ÷ monthly recovered value). ICE score = Impact × Confidence × Ease, each 1–10. Every fix is scoped so any competent vendor could build it. No obligation to build with Agent Lead Engine.

Fix 1: 90/60/30 renewal outreach cadence, maps to Leak 4

  • What it does: Pulls the renewal list from the AMS monthly, and starts a personalized email/text touch sequence 90 days before each renewal date (the X-date), review offer at 90, rate-check at 60, “your renewal is coming, we've already re-shopped it” at 30.
  • ICE: Impact 8 × Confidence 7 × Ease 9 = 504
  • Build cost: $2,000 one-time + $97/mo
  • Recovers: ~$22,000/yr
  • Payback period: ~1.1 months
  • Time to live: 10 days
  • What I need from you: AMS read access, 1 hour with whoever knows the renewal book.

Fix 2: AI receptionist with missed-call text-back, maps to Leak 1

  • What it does: Answers every call on the first ring, 24/7, books the appointment or takes a structured message, and texts the caller instantly so the conversation never dies. You get a summary by text and email.
  • ICE: Impact 9 × Confidence 8 × Ease 7 = 504
  • Build cost: $3,500 one-time + $297/mo
  • Recovers: ~$22,000/yr
  • Payback period: ~1.9 months
  • Time to live: 7 days
  • What I need from you: phone number porting decision, 30 minutes of FAQ answers.

Fix 3: Quoted-not-sold follow-up sequence, maps to Leak 2

  • What it does: Every quote automatically gets a 5-touch follow-up over 14 days (email + text), stops when the client responds, and hands warm replies to the producer. Nobody has to remember anything.
  • ICE: Impact 7 × Confidence 7 × Ease 8 = 392
  • Build cost: $2,500 one-time + $97/mo
  • Recovers: ~$12,000/yr
  • Payback period: ~2.5 months
  • Time to live: 7 days
  • What I need from you: quote register access, approval of message copy.

Fix 4: Website-to-AMS lead pipe, maps to Leaks 3 & 5

  • What it does: Web form submissions instantly create the AMS/CRM record, text the lead within 60 seconds with a booking link, and notify the office. No inbox, no spreadsheet, no re-typing.
  • ICE: Impact 6 × Confidence 6 × Ease 7 = 252
  • Build cost: $1,500 one-time + $47/mo
  • Recovers: ~$9,900/yr ($3,000 speed-to-lead + $6,900 re-keying)
  • Payback period: ~1.8 months
  • Time to live: 5 days
  • What I need from you: website form access, AMS credentials.
