About the audit

What does Agent Lead Engine do?
We find revenue leaks in independent P&C insurance agencies. Then, if you want help, we can build a fix. The audit comes first so you can see the problem before you buy a system.
What is the AI Revenue Leak Audit?
It is a 5-day review of the customer paths that matter, such as missed calls, web forms, quote follow-up, and renewals. You get evidence, a dollar figure for each finding, and a ranked fix plan.
How much does the audit cost?
The audit is $1,500 flat. The founding rate is $750 for the first 3 agencies. There are 3 founding spots open. The audit fee counts toward any build.
What is the guarantee?
If the audit does not find a leak worth more than the audit fee, we refund the audit fee in full. The report still shows what we checked and what we learned.
Do you need logins for the first probe?
No. We start with public entry points, such as the published phone number and website form. Read-only exports are optional and only used with your approval. No passwords are needed.
What if you find nothing?
You get the full audit refund under the guarantee. You also get the findings and evidence from the work completed.
What does the audit fee credit mean?
If you hire us for a build after the audit, the audit fee counts toward that build. Builds are priced from $2,500 to $5,000. The build is optional.
Who is this not for?
It is not for captive agencies, agencies with 25 or more staff, or teams that only want a software subscription before looking for a specific leak. It is for independent agencies that want a clear answer first.

What can happen next

What kinds of builds do you offer?
A build fixes a finding from the audit. Options include missed-call textback, quote follow-up, speed-to-lead, renewal countdown, after-hours receptionist, review engine, lead reactivation, and data sync. Builds are $2,500 to $5,000.
What is Guard?
Guard is optional monitoring after a fix is live. It includes a monthly money-recovered report. It is $297 to $497 per month.
Do you still build websites and CRM systems?
Yes, when that is the right fix. The front-office system from the old offer is one of the builds we can configure. It can include a website, pipeline, missed-call textback, lead follow-up, review requests, renewal reminders, and booking confirmations. We recommend it only when the audit shows it is useful.
What CRM platform do you use?
A build may use GoHighLevel when it fits the finding. We configure the account for insurance work and keep your contacts and data exportable. We will explain the setup before any build starts.
What automations can a build include?
Depending on the finding, a build can include new-lead follow-up, missed-call textback, review requests, birthday messages, renewal reminders, booking confirmations, and a pipeline for every lead.
What happens to my data if I stop?
Your contacts and agency data stay yours. We keep the setup structured so it can be exported if you stop using a managed system.
Will this work for independent or captive agents?
The offer is built for independent P&C agencies with 1 to 25 staff. Captive agencies are not the current fit.
